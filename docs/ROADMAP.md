# Murafiq — Product & Technical Roadmap

> **Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Phase Breakdown

### Milestone 1: Core MVP (V1.0) — Months 1–3
- PostgreSQL schema with complete RLS policies.
- Next.js 15 App Router platform with guided case submission.
- 9-state Case Lifecycle Machine with automatic 14-day & 30-day timers.
- AI Resolution Intelligence Engine: extraction, neutral summary, and PII redaction.
- Institution dashboard: response drafting & action plan checklist.
- Puppeteer server-side HTML-to-PDF report generator.
- Official WhatsApp Cloud API integration for PDF report dispatch.
- Closed pilot in Cairo with 10 private schools.

### Milestone 2: Hardening & Full Bilingual Launch (V1.1) — Months 4–5
- Full Arabic localization (RTL, Arabic typography, language switcher).
- Arabic PDF report generation.
- Response quality scoring for institutions.
- Email notifications via Resend.

### Milestone 3: Trust & Scaled Governance (V1.5) — Months 6–8
- Structural Resolution Quality Score (0–100 index).
- Formal institution dispute and verification escalation workflow.
- Expansion into educational tutoring centers and language schools.

### Milestone 4: Intelligence & Data Moat (V2.0) — Months 9–12
- RAG evidence retrieval from Egyptian Ministry of Education and regional policy benchmarks.
- Voice-to-text case intake for Egyptian Arabic voice notes.
- Predictive case resolution outcome modeling.
